# {{ title }}

I went to the Global Game Jam 2026 in Freiburg, Germany (at Pädagogische Hochschule Freiburg). This was my first-ever Game Jam for me and the experience has been unforgettable for me. 
I teamed up beforehand with a colleague of mine (also a programmer) and a fried of his (a really talented artist). 
The team at PH Freiburg did an amazing job organizing the event, making sure we have enough pizza to eat, a place to sleep and enough working space. 
We brought the full setup: laptops, monitors, keyboards, gamepads, crafting stuff and sleeping bags.

The topic (<b>Mask</b>) got announced at 5 PM on friday which meant we spent the entire evening brainstorming ideas for gameplay loops, art, style, concepts, etc. 
Fortunately, we were able to passionately aggree on a fast-paced FPS shooter (we were just going to somehow make it related to masks later, but we had some ideas). 
Our artist immediately started drawing some stuff: a brain-monster, a fist and a gun from the players perspective. 
When i saw that I was amazed at how cool it looks and i was ready to go all-in on the idea. Have a look at this incredible artwork:
