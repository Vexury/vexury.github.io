# Puzzle Hustle Support

Help for the Puzzle Hustle app on Android and iOS and the web version at
[puzzles.vexury.dev](https://puzzles.vexury.dev/).

## Contact

Write to **vexury.dev@gmail.com**. Every message gets an answer. For a
bug, say which device and system you play on (for example iPhone 15, iOS 26), which puzzle it
happened in, and if you can, add a screenshot.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Every puzzle, hint and the purchase work without one. Signing in (Profile tab, Sign in) is
optional and unlocks private groups with friends and keeps your progress in sync across devices.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Puzzles are generated on your device, so Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Hustle and every puzzle type
play without a connection. Groups and syncing need the internet.

**How do I move my progress to a new phone?**
Sign in on the old device, then sign in with the same account on the new one. Solved puzzles,
coins and equipped items come along. Unfinished boards and settings stay on the device they were
made on.

**How do hints work?**
You get one free hint a day across all puzzles. After that a hint costs 20 coins or a short video
that only plays after you confirm it. With "No Ads · Free Hints" every hint is free.

**What does "No Ads · Free Hints" do?**
It is a one-time purchase. Every hint is free, the coins of every new Daily, Weekly and Monthly you
solve are doubled, and the occasional ad between Hustle stages disappears. Nothing in the game
requires it.

**I bought it, but it is gone.**
Open the Profile tab and tap "Restore purchase" on the "No ads · Free hints" card. Use the same
Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. A purchase in the App Store does not carry over to
Android or the other way round, because each store handles its own purchases.

**Someone in my group has an offensive name.**
Tap their row in the standings and choose Report. Reports are reviewed by hand. If you own the
group, you can also remove the member.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Profile tab, Account, Delete account. Without the app, use
[puzzles.vexury.dev/delete-account](https://puzzles.vexury.dev/delete-account/) or write to the
address above. What is deleted is listed in the [privacy policy](/puzzle-hustle-privacy/).
