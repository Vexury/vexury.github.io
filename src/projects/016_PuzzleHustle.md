description: "A logic puzzle app for Android and iOS with ten puzzle types, from Nonogram and Sudoku to Zip and Shapes, based on the Prophecy Tablet from Honkai: Star Rail. Every puzzle is generated from a seed with exactly one solution, so daily sets and the endless Hustle mode are the same for everyone without a server, and the app works fully offline. Optional sign-in unlocks group leaderboards on a Cloudflare Worker. Solo project, out on Google Play and the App Store since October 2026 and playable in the browser."
