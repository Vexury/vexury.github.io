🧩 Puzzle Hustle

A logic puzzle app for Android and iOS with ten puzzle types: Shapes, Nonogram, Mosaic, Cats, Hearts, Sudoku, Sumdoku, Zip, Tracks and Slabs. Shapes is based on the Prophecy Tablet from Honkai: Star Rail.

Every puzzle is generated from a seed with exactly one solution, so the daily sets and the endless Hustle mode are the same for everyone without a server, and the app works fully offline. Optional sign-in unlocks group leaderboards on a Cloudflare Worker.

The apps are in closed testing. The web version on the right is the real thing, and your progress carries over to puzzles.vexury.dev. To sign in, use the full screen version.
