VexEngine

C++ · Vulkan · OpenGL

Bistro Interior · Glass spheres, path traced

My own rendering engine. Progressive path tracing on the CPU and on the GPU with hardware ray tracing, GGX materials, next event estimation with MIS, a binned-SAH BVH, a post-processing stack and a dockable editor.

how it is builtsource

Stylized Water

Unity URP · HLSL · compute shaders

Handwritten HLSL. Sea, lake and river share one shader and a baked water map. A compute ripple simulation draws wakes and rings that reflect at the shore, and a waterfall, an underwater view, rain and night sit on top.

Puzzle Hustle

React · Capacitor · Cloudflare Workers

A logic puzzle app with ten puzzle types. The daily set comes from a shared seed, so it is the same for everyone without a server, and the app works fully offline. Solo project, on Google Play and the App Store since October 2026.
